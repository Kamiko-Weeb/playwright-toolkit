Why this reads

  • One hero, huge. The crowned gold brainrot = "get rich hoarding." Readable at 150px.
  • The number sells it. A giant $/sec is the genre's #1 hook.
  • Face + eyes. Cartoon faces out‑click scenery in the recommendation grid.
  • Thick navy outlines keep text legible on any background at tiny sizes.

Roblox specs

  • Icon: 512×512, square. Keep near text‑free — the face + cash does the work.
  • Thumbnail: up to 1920×1080, 16:9. First one is the hero.
  • Keep key art out of the bottom‑right — the ▶ play button overlays there.
  • Export PNG. Test it shrunk to ~256px; if you can't read it, simplify.
